The problem

Your book is changing faster than your rating factors.

EVs, ADAS, and newer vehicle architectures have broken the assumptions in traditional GLM pricing models. The data exists, it just isn't reaching your actuaries in a usable form.

Combined ratios above 100%

The majority of motor books are running at a loss. Inadequate pricing at the risk level is a primary driver, not just claims inflation.

ADAS and EV blind spots

Advanced driver assistance systems and electric drivetrains create new risk profiles that current rating factors don't capture at the trim or variant level.

Data that sits in silos

Claims, telematics, and third-party feeds exist but aren't connected. Insurers are data-rich and insight-poor, and building in-house is slow and expensive.

Our approach

Cerevo applies machine learning to the policy and claims data insurers already hold, surfacing patterns that traditional approaches miss. We focus on a single problem, accurate risk pricing, and give it the depth that broad in-house programmes rarely achieve.
